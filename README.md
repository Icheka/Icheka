## What I'm building

A few things currently occupying brain cycles:

### 🐼 Tattoo Panda

Software for tattoo artists — from AI-assisted visualisation and body placement to stencil generation, print scaling, booking, and the less glamorous parts of actually running a tattoo studio.

`computer vision` · `generative AI` · `design tooling` · `SaaS`

### ◉ Orbit

Exploring privacy-first infrastructure for accessing medical testing and other health services in environments where discretion actually matters.

`healthtech` · `privacy` · `identity` · `logistics`

### ☀ Project Solar

Thinking about how software, financing, telemetry, and distributed energy systems can make reliable, green power less absurdly expensive.

`energy` · `IoT`

### ◈ Lucien

An experiment in giving AI systems something closer to organisational agency: context, tools, memory, coordination, and responsibility for outcomes rather than isolated prompts.

`agents` · `LLMs` · `infrastructure` · `future-of-work`
